How to read floor and ceiling counties

Look for the pattern at both ends. The ceiling counties have the highest ΔG. The floor counties are the most Republican baseline, where there were the fewest Democrats to move.

  • The ceiling, the single county with the highest ΔG, is Yuma at +13.05. Its S rose 8.89 points while its P fell 4.16.
  • The floor by ΔG, the single county with the lowest ΔG, is Yavapai at +4.15. Its S rose 2.29 points while its P fell 1.86.

Terminology

  • Ceiling counties are the five largest ΔG: Yuma +13.05, Greenlee +11.59, Santa Cruz +11.18, Graham +8.48, Pinal +8.26. Their ΔS: Yuma +8.89, Greenlee +5.34, Santa Cruz +4.63, Graham +5.24, Pinal +5.95. Their ΔP: Yuma −4.16, Greenlee −6.25, Santa Cruz −6.55, Graham −3.24, Pinal −2.31.
  • Floor counties are the most Republican baseline: the five lowest 2022 P.
