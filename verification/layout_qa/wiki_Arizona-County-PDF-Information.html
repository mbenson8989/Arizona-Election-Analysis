Arizona County PDF Information

A 3-page PDF covering all 15 Arizona counties, 2022 vs 2026*, modeled on Nebraska's 93-county summary PDF. Counties are sorted by 2026 Democratic share of active registration, highest first (Apache, 49.7%). Every page carries the 2026 UNOFFICIAL banner. Page 1 is the registration, vote and ballot table; page 2 gives the shares of ballots and registration; page 3 has the notes and sources.

Columns (page 1)

  • Dem Reg Share 2026 %: Democratic active registered voters ÷ all active registered voters, SOS November 2026 report, G.E. row. The sort column.
  • Dem Reg 2022 / 2026 (G.E.): Democratic active registered voters, G.E. row of each year's SOS State Voter Registration report.
  • Reg Change (number and percent): 2026 minus 2022, and that change as a percent of the 2022 figure.
  • Dem Gov Votes 2022 / 2026*: votes for the Democratic governor candidate, Katie Hobbs in both years (2022 official canvass; 2026 UNOFFICIAL).
  • Vote Change (number and percent): 2026 minus 2022, and that change as a percent of the 2022 figure.
  • Total Ballots 2022 / 2026*: total ballots cast in the county.
  • Ballot Change (number and percent): 2026 minus 2022, and that change as a percent of the 2022 figure.
  • Shading: blue = Democratic active registration rose (Pinal); peach = Democratic governor votes fell (Greenlee, Gila).

Columns (page 2): Democratic share of ballots 2022 and 2026* and the change in points; Democratic share of registration and total active registered voters for each year; registration-share change in points.

Statewide totals row: Democratic active registration 1,270,544 → 1,239,489; Democratic governor votes 1,287,891 → 1,434,824; total ballots 2,592,313 → 2,608,919; 2026 Democratic registration share 28.3%.

How the PDF was checked

Every cell was computed by two independent passes from the raw SOS files and then recomputed a third time by a separate checker that parses the raw files itself and compares against the PDF's own text at the displayed precision. The cell-by-cell log is verification/pdf_raw_crosscheck.csv in the repository.

Read this before using the numbers

  • 2026 figures are UNOFFICIAL until the Nov 23, 2026 statewide canvass. Cochise and Coconino show their county canvass as official on the SOS site; the other counties are unofficial.
  • Registration share and vote share measure different things. Unaffiliated and other-party voters, Republicans who voted for the Democratic candidate, turnout and candidate choice all move these numbers.
  • A change is not evidence of altered votes.
