Arizona Election Analysis: 2022 vs 2026* governor race, ΔG method

Method developed by Morissa Benson (CD3 Vice Chair, Nebraska GOP), from her Cedar County Audit on the Nebraska Election Analysis wiki. This repository applies the same arithmetic to all 15 Arizona counties. Read the wiki for the full write-up.

Measure 2022 2026* Change

Locked Arizona formula: ΔG = 3.30 + 1.29 × (drop in Democratic registration share, points); R² 0.47, n = 15. Implied extra votes, summed over counties: +137,207.
