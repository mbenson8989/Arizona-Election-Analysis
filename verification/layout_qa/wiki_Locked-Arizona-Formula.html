What it means: across Arizona's counties, each 1-point drop in Democratic registration share goes with about 1.29 points of ΔG, starting from about +3.30 when the share does not fall. The fit explains about 47% of the county-to-county differences in ΔG; with 15 counties the slope is imprecise (standard error 0.38).

Statewide check. At the pooled statewide drop of 2.33 points the equation gives ΔG +6.30; the pooled statewide ΔG is +7.64.

Worked example: Maricopa. P fell from 30.05 to 28.11, a drop of 1.9411 points. Predicted ΔG = +3.3019 + 1.2892 × 1.9411 = +5.80. Actual ΔG = +7.92*. Difference from the fitted line (actual − predicted) = +2.11*. This difference is not the line-11 residual in the template below, which is ΔG minus the registration-share drop (+5.97* for Maricopa).

The equation

  • S (Democratic vote share) = Democratic governor votes ÷ total ballots cast × 100. 2022: the canvass "Total Ballots Cast" row; 2026*: "Ballots Cast" on the SOS results site.
  • P (Democratic registration share) = Democratic ACTIVE registered voters ÷ all ACTIVE registered voters × 100, from the G.E. row of the SOS State Voter Registration report for each year.
  • G = S − P
  • ΔG = G(2026) − G(2022) = (S2026 − S2022) − (P2026 − P2022)
  • Reverse step (residual). Only where Democratic active registration fell: residual = ΔG − |P2026 − P2022|.
  • Solved backward: implied extra votes = D2026 − (S2022 ÷ 100) × ballots2026
  • Registration-scaled gap (the Nebraska wiki's "gap in votes" line, for comparison) = D2026 − D2022 × (DemReg2026 ÷ DemReg2022)

Why these files

  • Registration report. The SOS State Voter Registration report for each general election has an "Active" county table with an APR, P.E. and G.E. row. The G.E. row is the registration for the general election itself; its 2022 county totals equal the canvass "Total Eligible Registration" row, and its 2026 totals equal the "Registered Voters" shown on the SOS results site for every county.
  • Write-ins. Total governor votes include write-ins; they are used only in the over-vote check. S divides by ballots cast.
